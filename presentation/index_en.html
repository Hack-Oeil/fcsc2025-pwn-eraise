Challenge #551

eraise

It's up to you to get a raise!

This challenge is a service from a fictional CTF in Attack/Defense mode! Your team has set up the Shovel tool, which provides network-level visibility into incoming and outgoing traffic on your game VM.

For this service, your team got RCE'd at tick 0… Quite a bummer! It's up to you to understand the exploit from network frames and replay it against an opposing team.

To adapt to FCSC, only a single flagID is provided: KUHddCSYqwYFWowMzSxzdcHjCm3ayDFs.

The flag to find is in the format FCSC_.

Good luck!

- Shovel: http://localhost:8000 - Target service: nc localhost 4000
